road ahead.

Get a little closer to electric.

How long does an EV take to charge?+

There is no single charging time. Battery size, the charge window, available power, and the car's limits all matter. Use the calculator for a simplified AC planning estimate, and consult the vehicle and charger's specifications for your setup.

What should I check before charging at home?+

Check your vehicle's connector and AC charging limits, access to parking, and the electrical requirements of the charging equipment. A qualified installer can assess the supply and installation requirements for your property.

Is portable charging suitable everywhere?+

No. Compatibility and safe use depend on the power source, equipment, and local requirements. Follow the vehicle and charger manufacturer's instructions and check the suitability of the supply before use.

Does this website process orders or bookings?+

You can explore charging scenarios, browse the interior gallery, and save a charging estimate. The site does not take payments, reserve vehicles, or send booking requests.
